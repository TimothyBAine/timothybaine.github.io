About

I lead investor relations and data at XENO Investment Management, where I turn raw client and fund data into the numbers people decide with.

Outside work I'm completing an MSc in Financial Engineering at WorldQuant University. My research is on market regimes: when Hidden Markov Models describe markets well, when they break, and whether knowing the regime improves forecasts and portfolios.

I write about it at The Analytical Investor, where every post ships with tested code.
